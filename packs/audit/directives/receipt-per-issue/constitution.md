### receipt-per-issue

- **Directive**: Every tracked `receipts/*.md` file binds to exactly one GitHub issue. The filename matches `issue-<N>.md` or `issue-<N>-<slug>.md` (slug optional). No two receipts share an issue number. At the **completed-change** boundary a non-stub receipt in the change set must include `## What changed` (the outcome and significant behavior, with the work-item reference) and `## Verification` (commands/checks with their outcomes, or a durable evidence pointer). A fenced command with no outcome is not evidence; this check records that evidence was written, not that it ran. `## Decisions` / limitations is optional and omitted unless a meaningful tradeoff, deviation, or remaining limitation exists. Independent review (`## Audit`) remains required on those completed-change receipts. Session-only stubs (`## Session` or historical `## Accounting` as the only `## ` heading) are valid on intermediate commits and cannot satisfy a completed change.
- **Rationale**: Receipts are the durable post-implementation audit trace for work against a GitHub issue. Completeness belongs at the completed change (a PR's aggregate `base..HEAD`, or a commit made directly on the default branch), not at every intermediate commit — provenance stamping may still touch the receipt without narrative churn. The smaller contract (outcome + evidence, optional decisions) replaces copied checklists, filename inventories, mandatory empty sections, and mandatory slugs. Mechanical validation cannot prove a command ran or that an audit verdict is true; the scheduled lane re-adjudicates the `judge:` block.
- **Enforced by**: `.governance/packs/governance-kit/audit/directives/receipt-per-issue/check.sh`
- **Exceptions**: Per-receipt waiver — include `governance: allow-receipt-per-issue <reason>` in the first 10 lines. Reason required. Pre-existing receipts outside the current change set are grandfathered for the completed-change shape rules. Audit trail: `grep -r 'allow-receipt-per-issue' receipts/`.
